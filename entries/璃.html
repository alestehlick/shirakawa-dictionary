璃
Kun: tama, ruri  |  On: ri
precious stone ・ ruri ・ glass in compounds

Explanation

璃 combines 玉 with 離 as the phonetic ri. The jade classifier places the written word among valued stones and lustrous materials. Shirakawa's explanation follows the history of the name rather than proposing a separate ancient picture of this particular substance.

He cites the name of a Western Regions product in the Book of Han and connects 瑠璃 with an abbreviated Sanskrit transcription also found extensively in Buddhist texts. The sequence of sounds is therefore essential to the compound's history.

The same character appears in 玻璃, denoting glass. Material names of this kind do not always map neatly onto one modern mineral category. Shirakawa keeps the imported name, its Buddhist transmission, and the lustrous materials designated by the compound in view.
