旺
Kun: sakan  |  On: ou
vigorous ・ flourishing ・ bright ・ radiant

Explanation

旺 is semantic–phonetic, with the visible 王 supplying the sound ō. Shirakawa examines a fuller related phonetic that adds a departing foot above the head of an axe. He interprets that formation through a spirit-strengthening rite performed before setting out.

The rite adds spiritual force, and he accordingly finds meanings of vigour and strength among characters using this sound-family. This background, rather than the ordinary modern meaning king by itself, organizes his explanation of 旺.

Older glosses mention beautiful light and a halo around the sun. In the received word 旺, that radiant force becomes flourishing, thriving, or vigorous activity, as in 旺盛. Brightness and vitality remain linked through the idea of power manifested strongly.
