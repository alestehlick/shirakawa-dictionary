喉
Uncategorized
Kun: nodo  |  On: kou
throat ・ gullet ・ vital passage ・ strategic point

Explanation

喉 is semantic–phonetic: 口 identifies the bodily or vocal field, and 侯 supplies the sound kō. The older definition equates it with the throat, and 咽喉 remains a compound joining two closely related throat words.

Shirakawa suggests that the spoken word imitates the gulping sound produced when the throat is used. This is a proposal about the sound of the word, not a claim that 侯 pictures a throat.

Because the throat is a narrow passage indispensable to life, controlling it offers a model for controlling a crucial point. Classical language about gripping the throat therefore extends to dominating an essential route or position. The figurative strategic sense follows the vulnerability and necessity of the bodily passage.
