思
Kun: omou  |  On: shi
to think ・ thought

Explanation

The older form of 思 is 恖, with 囟 above 心. 囟 pictures the fontanelle, the place where an infant’s skull bones meet. Beneath it lies the brain, the organ that performs the work of thought. It also serves as the sound element.

Adding 心, the heart and mind, gives thinking or considering something inwardly. The upper form later became the familiar 田. 思 can describe careful reflection, but also what the heart dwells on: a wish, longing, affection, or concern for someone. Thought and feeling meet in the object held within the mind.
