楷
Uncategorized
Kun: kata, kaisho  |  On: kai
model ・ standard ・ correct form ・ regular script

Explanation

楷 is semantic–phonetic, with 木 and 皆 as the phonetic kai. Shirakawa notes that the older definition names a tree without securely identifying it. He treats the story about disciples bringing such trees to Confucius's grave as a legend, rather than reliable evidence for the character's origin.

For the relevant word-family he compares a term for wheat straw associated with firmness, suggesting a corresponding wood-related word. This is a proposed connection, not a complete botanical identification.

The established range includes a model, rule, proper form, and conformity to a standard. 楷書 names the regular script as one such formal style. The entry preserves both these familiar meanings and the uncertainty surrounding the earlier tree-name explanation.
