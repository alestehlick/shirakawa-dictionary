麗
Kun: uruwashii, urara  |  On: rei
beautiful ・ elegant

Explanation

The upper 丽 is the original form of 麗. It pictures the paired antlers of a deer; 鹿 was added below to identify the animal more explicitly. The balanced pair was admired for its beauty, giving ‘beautiful,’ ‘lovely,’ and ‘splendid.’

The two antlers also provide the sense of things standing side by side or forming a pair. That paired sense is preserved in 儷. A husband and wife can be described as a matched pair in 伉儷, joining companionship with the older image of two things set together. The character’s beauty is therefore closely connected with the visible balance of the deer’s antlers.
