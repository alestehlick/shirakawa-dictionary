罵
Kun: nonoshiru  |  On: ba
to curse ・ to revile ・ to abuse verbally

Explanation

罵 is semantic–phonetic, with 馬 supplying the sound ba. Shirakawa explains its net element through the related 詈, whose older definitions and those of 罵 refer to one another.

In his analysis, 詈 shows a net placed over the vessel containing an oath to a deity. The rite accuses and punishes a false oath. He proposes a similar ritual background for 罵, rather than a scene involving an angry horse.

He also discusses a Han text mentioning public punishment, cautiously reconstructing a procession and exposure of the offender. The wider point is that ancient punishment could operate through symbolic actions as well as physical force. From such condemning and curse-bearing acts develops the ordinary verbal sense of reviling or abusing someone.
