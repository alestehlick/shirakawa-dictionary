確
Kun: tashika, tashikameru  |  On: kaku
certain ・ sure ・ firm ・ to confirm ・ to ascertain

Explanation

確 combines 石, stone, with the sound element 隺. 隺 shows a bird about to take flight held back by a frame placed above it. The frame restrains it firmly, keeping it from moving away.

Adding 石 gives the hardness of stone and something made solid with it. Firm restraint and a hard material meet in the sense of being secure and unshakable. From physical firmness, 確 extends to something reliable, definite, or certain.

Japanese tashika describes that certainty, and tashikameru means to establish or confirm it. The action is to make sure that what is believed or reported has a firm basis.
