登
Kun: noboru  |  On: tou
to climb ・ to ascend ・ to offer ・ to present ・ to ripen

Explanation

登 combines 癶, two feet drawn together in readiness to move, with 豆, a tall-footed vessel. 豆 can also be understood here as a stepping stand. The feet are brought onto the raised support, giving ‘climb’ or ‘go up.’

A separate early form shows two hands lifting the vessel to make an offering. This contributes the sense of presenting or bringing something up before a superior or a god.

登 therefore includes both ascending and raising or presenting. Entering a position of authority is a kind of ascent, while adding to a quantity increases it. Uses for crops coming to fruition likewise describe something reaching its completed, productive state.
