頑
Kun: katakuna  |  On: gan
stubborn ・ obstinate ・ strong

Explanation

頑 combines 頁, the head, with 元 as its phonetic. 元 emphasizes the head and neck. The neck supports the heavy head with strength, providing the image of something firm and unbending.

Applied to a person's thinking or behaviour, that firmness becomes an unwillingness to bend. 頑固 is holding to one's position, and 頑 can describe obstinacy, dullness, or a stubborn refusal to respond.

The same strength can be valued in the body. 頑健 describes robustness and 頑強 a powerful resistance. Physical soundness and mental inflexibility are different judgments of the same firm condition.
