俺
Kun: ore, ookii, ware  |  On: en
large ・ full or ample ・ I in later usage

Explanation

俺 is semantic–phonetic, using 奄 as the phonetic en and 人 for the human sphere. Shirakawa connects 奄 with covering over something and places it with related words of similar sound.

The older definition of 俺 is large or ample. He compares the adjacent dictionary entry for 伴 and its relation to bodily fullness, showing that the earlier meaning differs from the familiar modern pronoun.

The first-person use appears later, notably in Yuan drama. Shirakawa records that change without offering a complete intervening history. The entry should therefore distinguish the older descriptive word from the later self-reference, rather than inventing a literal component scene in which a person announces an identity. Japanese ore belongs to that received pronominal range.
