中
Kun: naka, uchi  |  On: chuu
middle ・ center ・ inside ・ within ・ correct

Explanation

中 pictures a standard raised on a pole. Oracle-bone and bronze forms can show streamers above and below the central emblem. This is the flag of the central army, whose commander directed the force as a whole.

Early divinations use 中 for that central command. To stand at its head was to occupy the position from which the armies were brought under one authority. The centre is therefore both a location and an organizing point.

From this military emblem come the middle, the centre, and what lies within. The idea of occupying the proper middle also gives balanced, fitting, or correct. To reach that point is to hit the mark, producing uses for a successful shot, a correct result, or achieving the intended outcome.
