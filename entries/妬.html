妬
Uncategorized
Kun: netamu, sonemu  |  On: to
to envy ・ to be jealous ・ to begrudge

Explanation

Shirakawa classifies 妬 as semantic–phonetic. 女 identifies the traditional field of relations involving a woman, while 石 supplies the sound; he compares sound developments in other members of the 石 family. The phonetic is not being read as a literal stone placed in the heart.

The old dictionary definition centers on a wife's jealousy concerning her husband. Shirakawa then follows an extension to resentment of another person's ability or excellence, and to the wish to injure the person who possesses it. The ordinary meanings envy, begrudge, and be jealous belong to this widening emotional vocabulary.

He rejects a competing proposal for a 戸 phonetic because its historical pronunciation does not fit. His discussion of related words instead associates the feeling with destructive or injurious action. The female component reflects the gendered setting of the older definition, while the later verb applies more broadly to envy of other people.
