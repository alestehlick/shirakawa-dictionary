号
Kun: sakebu, naku  |  On: gou
number ・ designation ・ to cry out ・ to weep ・ to roar

Explanation

号 combines 口, the vessel holding written prayers, with 丂, a branch-shaped element. A loud cry or lament presses the appeal toward fulfilment. The written prayer is accompanied by the voice urgently calling upon the deity.

The fuller 號 adds 虎, a tiger, probably comparing the force of that cry with a tiger's roar. The word comes to embrace loud calling by people, birds, and animals. Modern 号 serves as the abbreviated writing.

From calling out come announcing, proclaiming, and naming. A person is identified by the name called, and the word extends to a title, designation, or sign. The familiar label and number develop from the audible act of calling something by its name.
