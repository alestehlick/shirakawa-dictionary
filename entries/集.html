集
Kun: atsumaru, atsumeru, tsudou  |  On: shuu
to gather ・ to collect ・ to assemble

Explanation

集 places 隹, a bird, above 木, a tree. The fuller form 雧 shows several birds together in the branches. Birds alighting in one place give the senses ‘gather,’ ‘assemble,’ and ‘come to rest.’ The shorter 集 lets one bird stand for the flock.

The character also has an old use meaning to bring something to completion or fulfilment, close to 就. This may preserve a connection with bird divination: the gathering and dispersal of birds could reveal whether an undertaking would succeed. An auspicious gathering would then signal that the matter was coming to fulfilment. In ordinary use, 集 retains the direct image of separate things coming together—a gathering of people or a collection brought into one place.
