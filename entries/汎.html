汎
Kun: uku, tadayou, hiroi  |  On: han, hon, hen
to float ・ to drift ・ extensive ・ general or universal

Explanation

汎 combines water with 凡 as the phonetic han. Shirakawa traces that phonetic to an older boat- or vessel-shaped form and an association with circling or moving about; it also supplies the sound in 風.

The older definition describes floating. A Book of Odes poem depicts two people borne along in a boat; Shirakawa cautiously suggests that its setting may involve water burial. That possibility belongs to his interpretation of the poem, not to every occurrence of 汎.

Drifting over water extends to breadth, distance, and being present throughout an area. 汎 overlaps with 泛 and 氾 in sound and meaning, although Shirakawa distinguishes their graphic histories. The broad or general sense follows movement spreading beyond a fixed position.
