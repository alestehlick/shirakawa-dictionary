入
Kun: iru, ireru, hairu  |  On: nyuu, ju
to enter ・ to put in ・ to insert

Explanation

入 pictures the entrance to a room. The early lines meet at the top and open below, marking the place through which someone goes inside. Enclosing this entrance within a roof produces 内, the interior.

入 names the movement across that threshold: to enter or go in. Used of something being moved, it means to put or bring it in; viewed from within, it can mean to receive or admit it. The short graph thus centres on entering, while 内 identifies the space reached by that movement.
