族
Kun: yakara  |  On: zoku
tribe ・ clan ・ family

Explanation

族 joins 㫃, the clan's banner, with 矢, an arrow used in swearing an oath. The participants pledge themselves beneath their common standard. They apparently made a gesture of breaking an arrow to mark the vow.

Those who shared the oath were the clan's members and its military body. Bronze inscriptions can direct someone to lead his 族 in protecting the king or describe several 族 being sent on campaign. Common allegiance and collective service give the group its unity.

From the assembled clan come family, lineage, companions of the same kind, and a gathering. 鏃 later uses 族 as its phonetic for an arrowhead. The banner and oath-arrow remain the earlier formation behind the social word.
