好
Kun: konomu, konomi, suki, suku  |  On: kou
to like ・ affection ・ beautiful ・ good

Explanation

好 combines 女, a woman, with 子, a child. It probably first expresses a mother cherishing her infant. The two figures place affection and care at the centre of the graph.

What is cherished is pleasing and dear. From the mother’s feeling toward the child come beauty, intimacy, and friendly affection. The meaning broadens to anything in a favourable condition: something good, agreeable, or desirable.

From finding something pleasing, 好 also becomes liking or taking pleasure in it. Its familiar uses for fondness and preference keep the personal attraction within the older scene of tenderness.
