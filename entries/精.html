精
Kun:  |  On: sei, shou
refined ・ pure ・ essence ・ spirit

Explanation

精 combines 米, grain, with the sound element 青. Its early meaning concerns selecting and preparing the finest grain. Rice and other cereals offered to the gods had to be carefully chosen and cleaned, so that the offering consisted of the best and purest part.

From this selected grain, 精 comes to describe whatever is refined, excellent, or free of coarseness. Careful selection also suggests exactness and thorough attention, giving ‘precise,’ ‘detailed,’ and ‘carefully worked.’

The best and most concentrated part of something is its essence. Applied to a person, 精 can therefore name the clear, vital power of the heart and mind, or the spirit itself. 精神 extends this sense of a concentrated inner vitality beyond the original offering of refined grain.
