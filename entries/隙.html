隙
Kun: suki, hima  |  On: geki, keki
gap ・ interval ・ crack or flaw ・ discord

Explanation

Shirakawa reads 隙 as a compound of 阜 and a graph showing jade radiance extending above and below. 阜 is the sacred ladder by which a deity descends. Jade placed at the divine arrival point manifests the spirit's presence through its light.

He therefore rejects a primary origin in a hole in a wall. As with 際, the setting is the meeting or boundary between divine and human. Light spreading from the jade gives the intervening space its significance.

From this opening come an interval, gap, crack, or flaw. An absence or breach can then describe discord between people. Shirakawa also compares 間 through sound and meaning while giving it a separate ritual formation; the related gap words need not share one literal wall-hole picture.
