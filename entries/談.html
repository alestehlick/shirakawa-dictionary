談
Kun: kataru, hanasu, hanashi  |  On: dan
to talk ・ conversation ・ discussion

Explanation

談 combines 言, speech, with 炎 as its sound element. It names ordinary talking: conversation, storytelling, and the exchange of remarks between people. Its uses include chatting with laughter and speaking playfully or in jest.

The related 譚 also concerns a story or an account, and the two graphs overlap in sound and meaning. 談 can therefore describe either the act of talking or the talk itself. The everyday conversation remains central, whether the subject is light or serious.
