程
Kun: hodo  |  On: tei
extent ・ degree ・ measure ・ distance

Explanation

程 combines 禾, grain, with 呈 as its sound element. 呈 shows a vessel containing a prayer raised in an offering to heaven. If its meaning also operates here, 程 probably first concerns praying for a good harvest.

The character also has early uses for a defined measure and for a very small unit of length. Its word family connects it with 正, ‘correct,’ and 定, ‘fixed,’ helping explain its meanings of a standard, a prescribed amount, or an established limit.

From a defined measure come degree and extent: how far something reaches or how much is appropriate. A measured course can also be a route, distance, or sequence of stages. 程 brings these uses together around an ordered, determinate range.
