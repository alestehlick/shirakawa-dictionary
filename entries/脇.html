脇
Uncategorized
Kun: waki  |  On: kyou
armpit ・ flank ・ side of the body ・ beside

Explanation

脇 is semantic–phonetic, using the repeated 力 element as the phonetic kyō and 肉 to identify the body. Shirakawa treats it as a variant of 脅, whose older definition concerns the two sides of the upper body.

The anatomical range includes the armpit, flank, and ribs. From the body's side the word also denotes a position alongside something. The different placement of the components in 脇 and 脅 does not create an independent original word.

Their later conventions nevertheless differ. Shirakawa specifically notes that 脇 is not normally used for the threatening meaning found in 脅迫. The entry therefore distinguishes shared formation and older sound from the practical division of meanings between the two received writings.
