銭
Uncategorized
Kun: suki, zeni  |  On: sen
hoe or cultivating tool ・ coin ・ money ・ levy or assessment

Explanation

銭 is the modern form of 錢, semantic–phonetic with 金 and 戔 as the phonetic sen. Shirakawa associates 戔 with thin, overlapping material.

The older word denotes an agricultural implement used for shallow cultivation or cutting weeds. A Book of Odes hymn about ceremonial farming names the implement among the tools to be prepared. Shirakawa distinguishes this light working of the ground from deeper ploughing.

Currency was subsequently cast in the shape of such tools, and the implement's name became a name for money. He compares the corresponding knife-shaped currency called 刀幣. The later senses of collecting, apportioning, or taxing concern that monetary range. His account therefore passes through tool-shaped currency rather than beginning with the round coin familiar from later history.
