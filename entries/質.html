質
Kun: naru, tadasu  |  On: shitsu, shichi, chi
quality ・ essence ・ substance ・ pledge ・ hostage ・ to question ・ to verify

Explanation

質 combines 斦, two 斤 or hand-axes, with a vessel whose later form resembles 貝. The vessel was originally 鼎, a bronze tripod. The axes cut an inscription into its side, recording an important agreement or oath.

質 first names this act of binding a promise through inscription and the inscribed contract itself. Related 則 and 劑 likewise concern stipulations cut into bronze vessels. The recorded terms establish what is to count as valid and can be used to test whether an action is right.

From this fixed basis come truth, verification, and the essential substance of a matter. An agreement can also require a pledge or security, giving goods placed in pawn and a person supplied as a hostage. These uses keep the force of something made definite and guaranteed.
