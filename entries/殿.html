殿
Kun: tono, dono  |  On: den, ten
palace ・ hall ・ mansion ・ lord

Explanation

The left side of 殿 is an earlier graph for 臀, the buttocks. It shows a person seated on a low stand, with that part of the body emphasized. 殳 adds a striking implement held in a hand. Together they probably record the custom of striking the buttocks.

The rear part of the body also supplies ‘behind’ or ‘last,’ including the rear guard of a group. The character has further uses for settling or bringing under control.

The familiar grand-hall meaning probably belongs to a related word concerning offerings laid before an ancestral representative and the place of worship. From that ceremonial hall come a palace, a noble residence, and the respectful titles tono and dono.
