時
Kun: toki, doki  |  On: ji
time ・ hour ・ season ・ calendar

Explanation

時 combines 日, the sun or day, with 寺 as its phonetic. 寺 is associated with possessing something and maintaining a condition; it is also the earlier writing behind 持, holding. What is maintained through time gives 時 its association with duration.

An older form joins 之 with 日. Its pointing or specifying function accompanies the sense of a designated occasion. The word's early range includes ‘this’ and waiting for the fitting moment.

The proper season was especially important in agriculture. Giving people the appropriate 時 meant supplying the calendar by which work could be timed. From duration and regulated occasions come a time, moment, season, or period, together with something happening from time to time.
