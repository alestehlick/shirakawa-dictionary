傲
Uncategorized
Kun: ogoru  |  On: gou
arrogant ・ to overawe ・ to despise ・ to act presumptuously

Explanation

傲 is semantic–phonetic, using 敖 as the phonetic gō and 人 for the human sphere. Shirakawa interprets 敖 as a long-haired person placed on a frame and struck.

He proposes that the victim is a ritual specialist and that the beating belongs to sympathetic magic, intended to transmit power against an enemy. This ritual identification is a reconstruction, but it explains why he connects the sound-family with threatening or overbearing force.

傲 then names the human attitude of attempting to dominate others through that authority: pride, arrogance, contempt, and presumptuous conduct. Related uses include overwhelming someone and noisy behaviour. The modern moral meaning is thus unfolded through Shirakawa's proposed coercive rite, rather than through a neutral picture of a person enjoying leisure.
