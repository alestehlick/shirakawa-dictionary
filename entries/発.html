発
Kun: tatsu  |  On: hatsu, hotsu, ho
to start ・ to set in motion ・ to depart ・ to emit ・ to occur ・ to reveal ・ to express ・ to discover ・ to invent

Explanation

発 abbreviates 發. The upper 癶 shows two feet set in the posture for departure. Below are a bow and the action of shooting it. The first arrow is released to announce the opening of hostilities.

That discharge initiates movement. From it come beginning, setting out, sending forth, and causing something to happen. An army departs, a sound is emitted, or a feeling rises from within and breaks into expression.

What has been concealed can likewise come into the open, giving discovery and disclosure. 発 joins the start of an action with its outward release. The full 發 preserves more of the shooting scene than the abbreviated modern character, in which the same range of beginning and manifestation continues.
