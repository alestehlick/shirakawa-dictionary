行
Kun: iku, yuku, okonau, michi  |  On: kou, gyou, an
to go ・ to act ・ to conduct ・ to perform ・ road ・ path

Explanation

行 pictures two roads crossing. Oracle-bone and bronze forms show the open ways meeting at a junction; 彳 and its counterpart are later parts of this complete road shape.

A road is where one goes, giving travelling, proceeding, and sending someone onward. Going through an action also becomes performing or carrying it out. A succession of people moving together can form a row or rank, supplying another branch of the word's uses.

Roads were also places where ritual power could be directed toward other territories. Characters such as 術 place a ritual object within the road formation, and many related graphs concern such acts. 行 consequently belongs to both ordinary movement and the ceremonial actions performed along a route.
