勾
Uncategorized
Kun: magaru  |  On: kou, ku
to bend ・ hook ・ to mark ・ to draw or seize

Explanation

Shirakawa treats 勾 and 句 as originally the same formation, subsequently distinguished in conventional writing. In 勾 he identifies the enclosing curve 勹 and the bent element 厶 as components of a curved shape. He suggests a connection with a body flexed for burial, so that detail should be understood as a reconstruction rather than a settled identification.

Bending and hooking provide the central range of meaning. Something curved can catch, draw, or hold another object; a hooked mark can also be made in writing.

Japanese compounds preserve several branches: 勾玉 names a curved bead, 勾引 concerns drawing or taking someone away, and 勾欄 refers to a railing. Their meanings are organized through the bend and its catching or enclosing action, rather than through an independent modern meaning assigned to each visible component.
