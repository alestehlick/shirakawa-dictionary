感
Kun: kanjiru, kanzuru  |  On: kan
feeling ・ to feel ・ emotion ・ sense

Explanation

感 combines 心, the heart, with 咸 as its phonetic. 咸 places 戉, a sacred axe, above the vessel holding written prayers. The axe protects the container and seals the prayer's efficacy within it.

With the prayer safeguarded in this way, the deity was thought to visit secretly during the night and answer it. 感 first concerns the divine heart being moved: the appeal reaches the god and draws a response.

The same relation extends to people. To 感 someone is to move their heart; to feel 感 is to experience the answering emotion. Gratitude, sorrow, recognition, and sensation belong to this wider range. The heart component makes inward responsiveness explicit, while related 撼 preserves the force of stirring or shaking.
