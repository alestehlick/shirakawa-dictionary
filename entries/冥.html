冥
Kun: kurai, fukai, haruka  |  On: mei, myou, men, ben
dark ・ unseen ・ deep ・ remote ・ the world of the dead

Explanation

冥 pictures the cloth laid over a dead person's face. Its lower strokes represent the hanging ties that fasten the covering. A ritual description specifies a dark outer surface, a red lining, and attached cords, matching the cloth and ties in this reading.

Covering the face marks the dead person's withdrawal from ordinary sight. From this funerary object come darkness and the unseen realm after death, then what is hidden, profound, remote, or beyond clear perception.

The related 幎 makes the cloth explicit, while 瞑 concerns closing the eyes. The cloth hides the face, while the closed eyes mark the end of ordinary sight. The physical covering and the unseen condition answer one another.
