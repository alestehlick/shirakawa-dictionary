車
Kun: kuruma  |  On: sha
vehicle ・ car ・ cart

Explanation

車 pictures a wheeled vehicle. The early forms show the body and axle with a wheel on either side; some also include the long pole by which the vehicle was drawn. As the picture became compact, the two wheels were reduced to strokes around the central frame.

The graph names the vehicle as a whole. Its basic sense continues in carts, carriages, and later vehicles, while the rotating wheel also allows 車 to name wheel-like mechanisms. The ancient forms preserve the construction of the vehicle more clearly than the squared modern character.
