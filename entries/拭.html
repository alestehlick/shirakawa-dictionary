拭
Uncategorized
Kun: fuku, nuguu, kiyomeru  |  On: shoku
to wipe ・ to wipe away ・ to cleanse

Explanation

拭 combines the hand classifier with 式 as the phonetic. Shirakawa interprets 式 through the ritual implement 工 and the action of purifying or warding off harmful influence. This gives a ceremonial background to the wiping action.

A ritual text describes wiping a jade tablet, while another account portrays Confucius wiping his tear-covered face with his sleeve. Together they show both formal cleansing and the ordinary removal of moisture.

Shirakawa places 拭 beside older wiping words written with 飾 and 刷. Their forms involve a cloth carried on the body, and their sounds are related. His analysis therefore links the manual action, the cloth used for it, and the clean condition achieved, rather than treating 式 only in its later sense of a rule or formula.
