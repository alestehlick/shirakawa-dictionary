陣
Uncategorized
Kun: tsuraneru, shiku  |  On: jin
military camp ・ battle formation ・ array ・ battle

Explanation

Shirakawa connects 陣 with 陳 and its older variants, whose meanings include arranging and setting out in order. The graph 陣 substitutes the recognizable 車, a vehicle or war chariot. He interprets the left-hand 阜 element through his concept of a sacred ladder or place of divine ascent and descent.

On that basis he proposes a military chariot stationed at a sacred place: the headquarters or principal camp. From a camp organized around such a position come arranging troops, battle formation, and the battlefield more broadly. He also relates the word's sound and sense to 陳 and 展, laying out and unfolding.

The proposal is explicitly limited by chronology. 陣 itself is not found in the pre-Qin texts he discusses, and Shirakawa says that its precise formative intention is difficult to recover. The camp interpretation is his reconstruction of the later graph, not a directly attested explanation from its creators.
