頼
Kun: saiwai, tayoru, tanomu  |  On: rai
divine favour or gift ・ good fortune ・ to rely on ・ to request or trust

Explanation

頼 is the modern form of 賴. Shirakawa identifies 剌 as its phonetic and associates that element with brilliant or forceful radiance. He consequently places the word's beginning in a benefit or favour received from Heaven.

The older gloss surplus concerns an additional gain, while classical passages also use the word for goodness and for depending on someone. Shirakawa compares 賚, a gift bestowed as divine favour, within the relevant sound-family.

A blessing becomes something on which a person may depend, giving the familiar reliance and trust meanings. He also records interchange with words for idleness and disease; these are separate related-word uses. The principal unfolding joins bestowed benefit, favourable condition, and dependence rather than deriving trust from the appearance of the simplified modern components.
