尻
Kun: shiri, soko  |  On: kou
buttocks ・ rear ・ anus ・ bottom

Explanation

尻 is semantic–phonetic, with 尸 providing the bodily context and 九 supplying the sound. Shirakawa points to older kō-type readings in related formations to explain the phonetic relationship.

The dictionary tradition compares several words for the buttocks, the flesh of the rear, and the adjoining thigh region. Shirakawa separates those overlapping anatomical names rather than assigning each modern stroke a bodily part.

The range of 尻 includes the anus and, more generally, the bottom or rear of something. He compares 孔 and 口 as related words for a small opening through which things pass. This sound-and-meaning group helps explain the bodily aperture, while the spatial bottom meaning follows the position of the rear itself.
