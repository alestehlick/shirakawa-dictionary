顔
Kun: kao, hitai  |  On: gan
forehead ・ face ・ countenance ・ facial appearance

Explanation

The fuller form 顏 combines 頁 with the phonetic 彥. Shirakawa reads 彥 through a forehead decorated with ritual body markings; 彡 expresses the beautiful colour or sheen of the decoration. 頁 supplies the person presenting a formal ritual appearance.

The original word in this account concerns the forehead, particularly the marked upper face displayed in an initiation ceremony. Classical glosses identifying 顏 with the forehead support that narrower starting point. From the marked area, the word broadens to the face and its visible expression or appearance.

Shirakawa also discusses personal and courtesy names that relate 顏 to groups associated with body marking, while noting that such customs existed within ancient China as well. Modern 顔 preserves the face meaning but largely hides the more particular ceremonial appearance through which he explains the fuller graph.
