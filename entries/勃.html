勃
Uncategorized
Kun: okoru, sakan, arasou, niwaka  |  On: botsu
to arise vigorously ・ to flourish ・ sudden outward force ・ to contend

Explanation

勃 is semantic–phonetic, combining 力 with 孛 as the phonetic botsu. Shirakawa interprets 孛 through a flower passing into the beginning of fruit formation, placing it among related graphs for successive stages of growth.

The added force element makes the outward pressure of generative strength central to 勃. Something stirs, swells into activity, or rises with energy. This underlies both flourishing and a sudden, forceful appearance.

He follows that force into expressions such as 勃然 and 勃興, where an emotion or movement springs up vigorously. Contention and disorder belong to another part of the same energetic range. The explanation therefore begins with growth pressing outward and develops into the abrupt manifestation of force, rather than reading 力 merely as a general instruction to be strong.
