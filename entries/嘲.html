嘲
Uncategorized
Kun: azakeru  |  On: chou
to mock ・ to tease ・ to ridicule ・ to jest

Explanation

嘲 is semantic–phonetic, with 口 indicating speech and 朝 supplying the sound chō. The older lexical definitions describe people making fun of one another through words.

Shirakawa compares 調 and another related writing used for teasing or playful treatment. These characters can overlap in the sense of verbal mockery, even though 調 also has other familiar uses. Their relevant sounds and meanings form the connection.

The range of 嘲 includes jesting, teasing, and sharper ridicule. His entry stays with this speech-based vocabulary rather than deriving the meaning from morning, the common independent meaning of 朝. The mouth classifier identifies the activity, while the phonetic links the graph with the established word for making sport of someone.
