詮
Uncategorized
Kun: sonawaru, akiraka  |  On: sen
to explain fully ・ to clarify ・ to expound ・ a clear principle

Explanation

Shirakawa classifies 詮 as semantic–phonetic. 言 marks speech, while 全 supplies the sound sen. The Shuowen's gloss gives the idea of being complete or fully provided: here, an account whose words and argument have been adequately set out.

He follows this into a classical use meaning to make a matter clear by discussing its grounds. The action is both thorough exposition and the clarification that exposition achieves. From it come explaining, instructing someone so that they understand, and an intelligible rule or way.

In the related word-family, Shirakawa also considers the selection and arrangement of words addressed to a deity. That comparison adds the context of carefully chosen, sufficient speech. The central unfolding remains complete discourse, a matter made clear through discourse, and the principle or understanding reached through that clarification.
