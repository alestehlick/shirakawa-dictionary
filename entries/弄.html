弄
Uncategorized
Kun: moteasobu, tawamureru, konomu  |  On: rou
to handle ・ to play with ・ to toy with ・ to perform music

Explanation

Shirakawa reads 弄 as a compound of 玉 and 廾, the pair of hands. The picture is of jade being held and handled with both hands. He places that object in an auspicious ritual setting rather than beginning with an ordinary child's toy.

The Shijing's house-blessing poem wishes that a newborn boy be given a jade object to handle. Shirakawa interprets this as invigorating the child's spiritual vitality. He compares early 保 forms showing a child accompanied by jade and protective clothing, associating the handled object with the child's continuing protection.

Because the object was kept with the child and repeatedly handled, the action develops into playing with or taking pleasure in something. Further uses include handling at will, manipulating, and playing music. The familiar casual or dismissive senses grow from that history of an object held, enjoyed, and moved in the hands.
