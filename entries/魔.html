魔
Kun:  |  On: ma
demon ・ devil ・ evil spirit

Explanation

魔 joins 鬼, a spirit or demon, with 麻 as the sound element. It was formed for the Buddhist rendering of Sanskrit Māra, the hostile power associated with obstruction and delusion. The new character uses a Chinese sound element to represent a borrowed religious name.

It belongs to the later vocabulary of Buddhism. From that setting, 魔 broadens to an evil being or harmful agency, and to the condition of a mind drawn into error.

The hemp meaning of 麻 is independent of its phonetic role here.
