鎌
Kun: kama  |  On: ren
sickle ・ harvesting hook ・ sharp edge or angle by related usage

Explanation

鎌 combines 金 with 兼 as the phonetic ren, a reading also found in 廉. Shirakawa interprets 兼 as holding two grain stalks together; 鎌 names the metal implement used to cut the gathered stalks.

The older dictionary defines it through another sickle word, and a regional vocabulary records it as a western name for a hooked harvesting tool. The agricultural implement therefore provides the concrete starting point.

Shirakawa compares related words for whetstones, sharpness, and acute angles. Through that family 鎌 can also overlap with 廉 in an edge or corner meaning. The account joins the tool's material, its phonetic connection with holding grain, and the sharp cutting quality that supports its extended uses.
