嵐
Kun: arashi  |  On: ran
mountain vapour ・ mist among mountains ・ strong wind ・ storm in Japanese

Explanation

嵐 is a compound of 山 and 風. Shirakawa begins with mountain air or vapour, including mist lying among the slopes. A poetic expression for green mountain haze preserves this atmospheric range.

The entry added to the older dictionary tradition calls it a mountain name, while another lexical source gives strong wind. Shirakawa records both strands rather than making the familiar Japanese storm sense the sole starting point.

Japanese uses 嵐 for arashi, a storm or violent wind. The character's history thus joins mountain atmosphere with wind, but its meanings have differed across contexts and languages. The modern components express the compound directly; the entry does not require a reconstructed sacrificial or divinatory origin.
