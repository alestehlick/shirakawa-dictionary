限
Kun: kagiru, kagiri  |  On: gen
limit ・ restrict ・ boundary

Explanation

限 combines 阜, the divine ladder, with 目, an eye, and 匕, a person turning away. The ladder marks the sacred approach by which the god ascends and descends. An eye possessing harmful, repelling power is displayed there to keep unwelcome beings out.

The person beneath the eye withdraws, unable to advance farther. This is the furthest point a person may reach, giving a limit or boundary. The graph marks the separation between the protected sacred place and the space outside it.

From this barred approach come limiting, preventing passage, and fixing an extent. The boundary may concern space, time, quantity, or what an action is permitted to reach.
