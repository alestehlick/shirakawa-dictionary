蓋
Uncategorized
Kun: futa, oou, kedashi  |  On: gai, kou
cover ・ lid ・ to cover over ・ introductory or conjectural particle

Explanation

蓋 combines 艸 with the phonetic 盍. Shirakawa interprets 盍 as a vessel with its lid, so the phonetic also contributes an association with covering. The older definition of 蓋 concerns grass used to roof or thatch a structure.

Thatched covering broadens to a cover or lid and to the action of placing something over another object. Shirakawa compares a family of words including 加, 架, and 駕, which likewise involve setting, mounting, or adding something above.

The grammatical use of 蓋 needs separate treatment. He explains one classical usage through a contraction or sound-based replacement of an expression equivalent to why not, while other contexts use it to introduce a statement. These function-word uses should not be narrated as though a physical lid naturally became every later particle.
