堕
Uncategorized
Kun: ochiru, kobotsu, yabureru  |  On: da, ki
to fall ・ to collapse ・ to break down ・ to neglect by related usage

Explanation

堕 is the modern form of 墮, with 隋 as the phonetic da. Shirakawa interprets 隋 through portions of sacrificial meat piled before the sacred ladder used for divine ascent and descent. 墮 describes that heap collapsing or falling down.

He distinguishes another traditionally conflated form involving ritual implements and a curse. That graph may explain the abandonment or destruction meanings and its different ki reading. He questions treating all the received forms and readings as one transparent original character.

The main 墮 branch is falling and collapse, with related vocabulary including 墜. Interchange with 惰 adds ceasing or neglecting an activity. Modern moral deterioration in 堕落 belongs to this falling range, while Shirakawa preserves uncertainty about how some of the older destructive uses were combined.
