舷
Kun: funabata  |  On: gen
side of a boat ・ gunwale ・ ship's side

Explanation

舷 is semantic–phonetic: 舟 identifies a boat, and 玄 supplies the sound gen. The word denotes either side of the vessel, especially the edge or gunwale along which a person can sit or strike.

Shirakawa cites Guo Pu's Jin-period rhapsody on the river, where people sing while beating the side of a boat. He presents this as the earliest usage example in his discussion. It gives a concrete setting for the part named by 舷.

The entry offers a lexical and phonetic explanation rather than a reconstructed ancient ritual scene. The boat component specifies the object, while the sound component distinguishes this particular part of it; the familiar ship-side meaning remains the central one.
