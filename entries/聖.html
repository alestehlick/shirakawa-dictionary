聖
Kun: hijiri  |  On: sei, sho
saint ・ sage ・ holy person

Explanation

聖 combines 耳, an enlarged ear, with 口, the vessel containing written prayers, and 𡈼, a person standing on tiptoe. The figure recites the prayer and strains upward to hear the deity's answer. The prominent ear makes this exceptional hearing visible.

The 聖 person can receive a revelation beyond ordinary perception. In an old story, the musician 師曠 listens to the wind and discerns the outcome of a coming war. Such perception belongs to the sacred specialist's insight.

From this come extraordinary understanding and mastery. 聖 becomes the sage, the highest ideal of knowledge and virtue, and can honour a ruler or someone supremely accomplished in an art. The later distinction develops the power to hear what others cannot.
