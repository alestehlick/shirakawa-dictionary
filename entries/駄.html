駄
Kun:  |  On: da, ta
packhorse ・ to load ・ useless ・ inferior

Explanation

駄 is a later writing of 馱, formed from 馬, horse, with 大 as the phonetic. Its original action is loading goods onto a horse's back. The horse element supplies the carrying animal, while the second element supplies the sound.

The character can name the load, the act of carrying it, or the packhorse itself. 駄賃 is the payment for this transport, and 駄 can count loads. These uses remain close to the practical work from which the word starts.

The related 佗 also concerns bearing a burden, and the camel name 駱駝 belongs to the same carrying vocabulary. Japanese later also uses 駄 for something poor, wasted, or unproductive.
