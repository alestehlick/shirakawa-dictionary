貴
Kun: tattoi, toutoi, tattobu, toutobu  |  On: ki
precious ・ noble ・ valuable ・ high-ranking

Explanation

貴 combines a pair of hands with 貝, a cowry shell. The hands lift and present the shell, showing the care given to a precious object. The upper element represents the two hands together.

Cowries circulated as valuables and currency. They could be strung into counted sets, and bronze-vessel inscriptions record substantial expenditure in such strings of shells. Their worth gives the concrete basis for 貴: something costly and highly valued.

The same valuation extends from objects to people. 貴 can indicate high rank, generous emoluments, or an elevated and admirable character. To regard someone or something as 貴 is to prize or honour it.
