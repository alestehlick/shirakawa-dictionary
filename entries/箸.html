箸
Kun: hashi, taru  |  On: cho, chaku
chopsticks ・ bamboo tube ・ to attach or become evident by related usage

Explanation

箸 is semantic–phonetic, with 竹 identifying bamboo and 者 supplying the sound cho, also represented in 楮. The older dictionary identifies the implement used with food, and Shirakawa notes a later alternative writing used in the same chopstick vocabulary.

The principal meaning is the pair of eating sticks. The lexical range also includes a bamboo tube or container, retaining the material indicated by the classifier.

箸 can interchange with 著 for attaching, becoming evident, and related meanings. Those uses belong to another branch of the shared sound-family; they should not be explained as actions performed by chopsticks. Shirakawa's brief account distinguishes the bamboo utensil from these conventional exchanges between related written forms.
