二
Kun: futa, futatsu  |  On: ni, ji
two ・ second

Explanation

二 places two horizontal counting marks together. Like the earliest forms of 一, 三, and 四, it records the number by repeating the basic tally stroke. The visible pair gives the numerical sense directly.

Two can also mean a second occurrence, a pair, or a doubling. Where unity is expected, a second allegiance becomes divided loyalty or opposition, giving uses for hesitation and turning away. Later philosophical schemes associate two with earth and yin; those associations build on an existing numeral.

Formal documents often use 貳, now 弐, to prevent the simple numeral from being altered. The more elaborate graph also has a history involving paired or duplicate records, which suits the distinction between a primary and a second counterpart.
