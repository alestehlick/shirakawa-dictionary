芯
Uncategorized
Kun: hosoi  |  On: shin
core ・ pith ・ wick ・ rush used for lamp wicks

Explanation

Shirakawa identifies 芯 as a semantic–phonetic character: the plant component indicates its original botanical setting, while 心 supplies the sound shin. The word first names a kind of slender rush, the plant used for lamp wicks. This botanical usage is the concrete starting point of his explanation.

The later sense of a core or something at the center develops through the close association with 心. What lies within a plant, wick, or other object can be described as its central substance, and 芯 consequently comes to designate the material core rather than the whole plant.

Shirakawa places the written word in texts from the Tang and Song periods onward. Its formation should therefore not be presented as a securely identified oracle-bone picture. The account follows a comparatively late plant word, its phonetic connection with 心, and its widening use for the inner part of things.
