虹
Uncategorized
Kun: niji  |  On: kou
rainbow ・ male rainbow in older classification ・ rainbow-coloured banner

Explanation

虹 is semantic–phonetic, with 工 supplying the sound kō. Shirakawa also sees its spanning, slightly curved form as appropriate to the body of the rainbow creature. The creature was imagined with heads at both ends.

The older definition treats the rainbow as a dragon-like being, explaining the 虫 classifier. Traditional classification distinguishes the male 虹 from the female 霓. This is an ancient conception of the phenomenon, not a zoological claim.

Shirakawa questions a transmitted older variant using the lightning-shaped 申 because it does not fit this formation. Rainbow-coloured flags continue the visible phenomenon, while other meanings such as bridge or contention depend on sound-related interchange. The central explanation remains the spanning, two-headed celestial creature.
