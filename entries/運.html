運
Kun: hakobu  |  On: un
to carry ・ to move ・ fortune

Explanation

運 combines 辵, movement along a road, with the sound element 軍. The early 軍 shows a flag streaming above a war chariot. The general’s flag directed the army: its movements told the other chariots when to wheel, advance, or change position.

運 expresses this setting of the army in motion. Turning and moving extend to circulating, carrying, and conveying something from one place to another. The shared idea is movement directed along a course. Applied to the course of events, the character can also mean the way circumstances turn or the lot that falls to someone—hence fortune or destiny. The practical uses for transport retain the more immediate act of moving something to its destination.
