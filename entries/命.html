命
Kun: inochi, iitsuke, sadame  |  On: mei, myo
life ・ command ・ order ・ fate

Explanation

命 combines 令 with 口, the ritual container holding written prayers. 令 shows a kneeling figure wearing a ceremonial cap and receiving a divine revelation. Adding the prayer vessel makes explicit the appeal and the answer bestowed in response.

The original sense is a divine command or gift. Early inscriptions use 令 for the same word. Western Zhou bronzes speak of receiving Heaven's great mandate, making this bestowal a principle of the dynasty's founding.

A person's life and its allotted length were also gifts of Heaven, so 命 names life, destiny, and what lies beyond human control. The word extends to a ruler's orders and to commands more generally. Divine allotment and human command share the authority of something received and binding.
