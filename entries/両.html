両
Kun: futatsu  |  On: ryou
pair of wheels ・ two ・ both ・ unit for weight or other quantities

Explanation

両 is the modern form of 兩. The earlier graph pictures the two wheels of a vehicle side by side. Bronze inscriptions use it for a vehicle, and it is the original writing behind 輛, the word used in counting vehicles.

The paired wheels give two things taken together, both members of a pair, or things standing alongside one another. The emphasis is on correspondence within a set of two.

兩 also became a unit in systems of counting and measurement, including weight and later money. These uses broaden the numerical range of the word, while the original form remains the pair of wheels that belong to one vehicle.
