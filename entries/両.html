両
Uncategorized
Kun: futatsu  |  On: ryou
pair of wheels ・ two ・ both ・ unit for weight or other quantities

Explanation

両 is the modern form of 兩. Shirakawa reads the earlier graph as a pair of wheels set alongside one another and treats it as an initial writing of 輛, the vehicle-counting word. Bronze inscriptions use two transmitted variants for vehicles.

He therefore questions an older dictionary arrangement that separates a repeat or twice graph from the weight unit 兩 while leaving the first shape unexplained. In his account the bronze evidence points back to one vehicle-related formation.

From paired wheels come two, both, and things placed in a corresponding pair. The word also serves as a unit in several systems of counting or measurement, including weight. Sound-related uses such as 倆 concern other branches of the vocabulary; the central pictorial explanation remains the paired wheels.
