説
Kun: toku, tokasu, tokeru  |  On: setsu, zei
to explain ・ to persuade ・ theory ・ doctrine

Explanation

説 combines 言, solemn words addressed to the gods, with the sound element 兌. In 兌, 兄 is a ritual specialist praying before the divine presence. The 八 above marks a faint descent of divine breath in answer to the prayer. The worshipper receives it and enters a rapt, possessed state.

説 first concerns pleading or explaining one’s request to the god and receiving the divine response. From addressing the gods, the meaning broadens to addressing people: stating a case, persuading, explaining, or instructing.

The joy of receiving the divine presence is also written 悦, with 心 added to 兌. 説 can share that sense of gladness or becoming at ease, while its ordinary uses centre on putting something into words so that another can understand.
