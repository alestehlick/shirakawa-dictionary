箋
Kun: harifuda  |  On: sen
label ・ slip of paper ・ letter ・ annotation

Explanation

Shirakawa treats 箋 as semantic–phonetic. The bamboo classifier supplies the writing-material category, and 戔 provides the sound sen together with an association with thin things laid over one another.

The Shuowen's definition concerns writing placed on the outside of something, as on a title label. A narrow writing slip or attached identification therefore gives the concrete starting point. From such a slip come a label, a name tag, and a piece of writing.

Shirakawa distinguishes its conventional uses from 牋, which more often concerns written memorials or a literary form. 箋 is especially used for attached notes and explanatory comments, as in 箋注 and 附箋. The physical addition of a small written slip thus supplies the bridge into the scholarly action of annotating a text.
