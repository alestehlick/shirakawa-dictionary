枕
Uncategorized
Kun: makura, nemuru  |  On: chin, shin
pillow ・ to rest one's head ・ to lie down ・ to place across

Explanation

枕 is semantic–phonetic, with 木 and the phonetic also used in 沈 and 鴆. Shirakawa interprets that phonetic's earlier shape as a person lying with the head supported.

The older definition describes the object placed beneath the head when reclining. A Book of Odes elegy mentions an adorned pillow beside the person in the coffin, showing the object in a funerary setting as well as ordinary rest.

The noun becomes a verb for using a pillow, lying down, or remaining at ease. Other uses concern laying something across or placing it against the neck. Shirakawa compares 沈 through the related notion of lying or settling down, so the word's extensions remain connected with a supported or horizontal position.
