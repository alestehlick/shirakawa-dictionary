人
Kun: hito  |  On: jin, nin
person ・ human being

Explanation

人 depicts a human figure seen from the side. The early oracle-bone and bronze forms show the body with an arm and a leg in profile; the modern two-stroke shape preserves this compact outline. It differs from 大, which presents the body frontally with limbs spread.

This side-view figure recurs in characters concerned with the body, including 身 and 包. Its basic meaning is a person or human being, extending collectively to people and a population. It can also refer to another person and, through a person's conduct and qualities, to character or personality. The graphic starting point remains the individual human figure.
