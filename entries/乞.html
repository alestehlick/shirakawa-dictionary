乞
Kun: kou, motomeru  |  On: kitsu, kotsu
to pray ・ to ask for ・ to beg ・ to receive or give

Explanation

Shirakawa reads 乞 as an early pictorial form of 气, showing moving cloud vapour. 气 in turn precedes the fuller 氣. The older dictionary discusses 气 under cloud air without providing a separate 乞 entry.

乞 concerns a rite of looking toward the clouds and praying. Bronze inscriptions asking for long life preserve the act of petitioning a deity. Asking or begging therefore follows the prayer directed toward a divine source.

The word can concern receiving something and, in another direction, granting it. Shirakawa compares related prayer words through sound and meaning. His account begins with cloud-directed petition, rather than a modern picture of an impoverished person, and distinguishes that family from other similar-looking graphs with different histories.
