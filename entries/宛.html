宛
Uncategorized
Kun: ateru, kagamu, magaru, fukuyoka, sanagara, atakamo  |  On: en, utsu
to bend ・ rounded and graceful ・ just as ・ as though

Explanation

宛 is a compound in Shirakawa's analysis. 宀 is the ancestral sanctuary, and the lower element shows a seated person with bent, rounded knees. He proposes that the person is sitting within the sanctuary and revering a deity.

He rejects the traditional picture of someone bending grass over the body as a covering. The visible posture, not a grass shelter, gives bending and curved fullness. Related 婉 and 腕 share the association with rounded or gently bent form.

The range broadens to graceful appearance and to remaining just in a particular form, hence just as or as though. Another reading overlaps with 鬱 in a constrained condition. Shirakawa's central sequence follows the seated posture into curvature, fullness, and appearance.
