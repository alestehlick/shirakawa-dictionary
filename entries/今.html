今
Kun: ima  |  On: kon, kin
now ・ the present

Explanation

今 pictures the lid or stopper of a jar. The early shape is the closing piece itself. A stoppered wine vessel appears in related formations, including early writings connected with drinking, which preserve the concrete use of the lid.

The character was borrowed for a similarly sounding word meaning the present time. This use became dominant, giving ‘now,’ ‘today,’ and the immediate occasion. A temporal 今 stands beside spatial 近: each concerns what is close, one in time and the other in place.

Other members of the 今 sound family preserve the idea of closing or covering. That material history explains the graphic form, while the familiar time word depends on the loan of its sound.
