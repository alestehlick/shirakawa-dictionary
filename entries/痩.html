痩
Kun: yaseru, hosoi  |  On: sou
to become thin ・ to waste away ・ slender

Explanation

Shirakawa explains the fuller form 瘦 as semantic–phonetic, with 疒 indicating bodily condition and the sō phonetic associated with an elder. 痩 is the simpler received Japanese form.

The seal form uses an earlier version of that phonetic. In Shirakawa's reconstruction it represents an elderly person carrying fire inside the ancestral sanctuary during a ceremony. The elder's age is relevant to the word's association with becoming lean and worn.

The older definitions concern thinness, whether caused by advanced age or by illness. From wasting away the word also describes a narrow or slender appearance. Shirakawa's account keeps the phonetic's ritual history separate from the bodily classifier while connecting the older person's frailty with the established meaning of 痩.
