毒
Kun: sokonau  |  On: doku
poison ・ to poison ・ harm

Explanation

毒 depicts a woman richly adorned for ritual service, especially the abundance of ornaments in her hair. Related forms show the dressed hair from the side or add a hand attending to it. The striking feature is the thickness and elaborateness of the decoration.

This gives the older sense of something ample or ‘thick,’ including generous or wholehearted treatment. 篤 shares that sense through a related word. Elaborate adornment can also suggest excess in contrast with simplicity.

The familiar poison sense is probably a sound loan from another old writing. Once established, it produces uses for poisonous plants or medicines, harming, painful intensity, cruelty, and bitterness.
