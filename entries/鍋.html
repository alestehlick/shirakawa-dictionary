鍋
Kun: nabe, karimo, aburasashi  |  On: ka
round-bottomed pot ・ cauldron ・ axle fitting ・ oil container in older usage

Explanation

鍋 combines 金 with 咼 as the phonetic ka. Shirakawa associates 咼 with a rounded, swirling form, relevant here to a pot or cauldron with a circular bottom.

The older range is not restricted to cookware. He records a wheel-related fitting and the container used to oil a vehicle, as well as a sickle usage that he tentatively connects with hammering or working metal.

The familiar Japanese nabe belongs to the round cooking-vessel branch. The metal classifier identifies the material, while the phonetic supplies both the sound and the rounded-form association in his account. The several older implement meanings should be retained as lexical evidence rather than forced into a single story about preparing food.
