瘍
Uncategorized
Kun: dekimono  |  On: you
sore ・ ulcer ・ wound ・ swollen lesion

Explanation

Shirakawa treats 瘍 as semantic–phonetic, with the illness component supplying the bodily condition and 昜 providing the sound yō. The Shuowen defines a wound of the head, supported by a classical example of a sore appearing there.

He broadens the reference through the Zhouli's office of the 瘍医, whose responsibilities include treating swellings and lesions generally. The word's use is therefore not confined to the scalp.

昜 belongs to the vocabulary of sunlight and brightness. Shirakawa tentatively connects its contribution here with skin damaged as though by strong sunlight or burning. He also compares 痒 as a historically related writing with overlapping meaning. The account combines the sound element, old examples of wounds, and the wider class of sores and swollen lesions treated under this name.
