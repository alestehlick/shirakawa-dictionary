玩
Uncategorized
Kun: moteasobu  |  On: gan
to handle familiarly ・ to play with ・ to treat lightly ・ to take pleasure in

Explanation

玩 combines 玉, jade, with 元 as the phonetic gan. Shirakawa also notes an older variant using 貝. He follows the older definition's connection with 弄 through objects placed close to a newborn child.

The Book of Odes describes gifts of jade to a boy and earthenware to a girl. In Shirakawa's interpretation, such jade, cowries, and pottery had a protective or spirit-strengthening role. Repeatedly keeping and handling these objects provides the background to familiar handling and play.

That familiarity can become excessive or disrespectful: to play with something may mean to make light of it or profane it. Other uses emphasize enjoyment and indulgence. The sequence thus runs from close handling of a valued object to play, pleasure, and the dismissive treatment possible when reverence is lost.
