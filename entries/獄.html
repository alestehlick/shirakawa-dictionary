獄
Kun: hitoya  |  On: goku
prison ・ trial ・ lawsuit

Explanation

獄 combines 言 with 㹜, two dogs. 言 shows a prayer vessel with 辛, a tattooing needle, above it. It represents an oath before the gods: if the statement is false, the speaker accepts tattooing as punishment. The two dogs are the sacrificial animals presented by the opposing parties in a lawsuit.

Offering the dogs and swearing the oath formally begin the trial. The dispute is placed before divine judgement, with each party bound by the sacred declaration. 獄 therefore first concerns adjudication and legal proceedings.

A person who loses the case may be found guilty and confined. From the trial, 獄 comes to name the place of imprisonment and, more broadly, punishment or guilt. The prison sense grows from the judicial rite shown in the graph.
