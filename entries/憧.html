憧
Kun: akogareru  |  On: shou, dou
unsettled in mind ・ absent-minded ・ to long for ・ foolish by related usage

Explanation

憧 is semantic–phonetic, with 心 identifying the mind and 童 supplying the sound. Shirakawa points to the shō reading in 鐘 and related characters as evidence for this phonetic range alongside dō.

The older definition describes an intention that will not settle. The mind moves impulsively, wanders, or remains vaguely absorbed. Shirakawa connects this with 衝 and related movement vocabulary; the foolish or clouded sense involves another sound-related use.

In 憧憬 the attention reaches toward something distant, giving the familiar Japanese meaning of yearning or aspiration. The account begins with unstable, roaming thought rather than treating admiration as the original meaning. Read with 憬, it also preserves the difference between this Japanese compound's later usage and the older individual words.
