奈
Kun: karanashi, nanzo  |  On: na, dai, nai
how ・ what can be done ・ crabapple ・ phonetic element in names

Explanation

奈 is a popular abbreviated writing of 柰, the name of the karanashi, a crabapple-like fruit tree. 柰 has 木 above 示 and is conventionally classified as a phonetic compound, with 示 as its proposed sound element. The sounds do not match satisfactorily, however, so that part of the formation remains unresolved.

The questioning use appears in 柰何, later 奈何: ‘how?’ or ‘what can be done?’ It corresponds to 如何, with related historical sounds, and belongs beside interrogative 那. This branch is explained through the words' use and sound relationships.
