藍
Uncategorized
Kun: ai  |  On: ran
indigo plant ・ plant used for blue dye ・ indigo colour

Explanation

藍 is semantic–phonetic, with 艸 specifying a plant and 監 supplying the sound ran. Shirakawa points to 濫 and 覧 as other examples of that phonetic's ran reading.

The older definition identifies a plant used to dye cloth. A Book of Odes poem describes gathering it, while the Rites of Zhou assigns officials responsibility for collecting dye plants and distributing them to dyers. These passages place 藍 within an organized practical craft.

The plant name consequently also denotes the colour obtained from it. Shirakawa notes the well-known expression about blue coming from indigo in Xunzi. The history proceeds from the dye-bearing plant to its processed colour, with the classifier and phonetic keeping their separate roles in the character's construction.
