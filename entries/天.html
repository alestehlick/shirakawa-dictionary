天
Kun: sora, ame, ama, amatsu  |  On: ten
heaven ・ sky ・ nature ・ divine will ・ innate

Explanation

天 draws attention to the head of a frontal human figure, 大. In the earliest forms the head can be enlarged into a clear round or broad shape; the later upper horizontal stroke preserves that emphasis. The head is the body's highest point.

The word belongs beside terms for the crown, summit, and forehead. This relation provides the connection with what is overhead: the sky and heaven. The shape begins with the marked human head, while its meaning reaches the great space above people.

Heaven also becomes the divine authority to which human rule is answerable, as in 天命, the heavenly mandate. Further uses concern nature, what comes from heaven, and what is given at birth.
