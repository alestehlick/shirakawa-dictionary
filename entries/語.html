語
Kun: kataru, katarau, kotoba  |  On: go
language ・ word ・ to tell ・ to speak

Explanation

語 combines 言, words, with the sound element 吾. In 吾, a wooden cover is placed over a vessel containing a prayer. The cover protects the prayer and preserves its power against interference. 語 names the words spoken for that protection.

Sacred words could attack or defend. 言 carries the force of a solemn assertion or oath that acts against another power; 語 answers with words that guard the prayer. Such utterances could also address and pacify the spirits of a place when people settled there. Speech begins here as an effective exchange with the unseen world.

The meaning later broadens to words and language in general, then to speaking with another person, telling a story, explaining, or giving instruction. 言語 joins the two old kinds of utterance in the ordinary word for language.
