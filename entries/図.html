図
Kun: hakaru  |  On: zu, to
map ・ diagram ・ illustration ・ plan ・ to plan ・ to draw ・ book ・ document

Explanation

図, formerly 圖, places 啚 inside 囗, the boundary of an area. 啚 combines the granary form 㐭 with a surrounding region; it is also the earlier graph behind 鄙. 圖 shows a map of cultivated land with the granary’s location marked within it.

The map made the arrangement of fields and storage visible. It could therefore serve as the basis for managing the land and planning its use. From this come a plan, a scheme, and the act of considering how something should be carried out.

The visible representation also broadens to a drawing, diagram, or illustration. The map remains the central image: an area understood and organized by setting its important places down in a form that can be inspected.
