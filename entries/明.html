明
Kun: akari, akarui, akarumu, akaramu, akiraka, akeru, aku, akuru, akasu  |  On: mei, myou
light ・ bright ・ clear ・ dawn ・ open ・ reveal

Explanation

The older form of 明 is 朙, combining 囧, a window, with 月, the moon. It pictures moonlight entering through the opening. The left side later took the familiar shape 日, but the ancient forms preserve the window.

The place where light entered was also a place for welcoming and worshipping the divine presence. In the loess regions, dwellings could have rooms cut around a sunken central court, with light entering from the side facing that opening. Light and the presence of the gods were closely joined in this setting, as the compound 神明 preserves.

From illumination come ‘bright’ and ‘clear.’ Something made visible becomes evident or understandable; the coming of light also gives the sense of daybreak. 明 can thus describe light itself, clarity of understanding, or the act of bringing something into the open.
