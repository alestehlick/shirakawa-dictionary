恣
Kun: hoshiimama  |  On: shi
to follow one's feelings ・ unrestrained ・ arbitrary ・ to leave to someone's will

Explanation

恣 is semantic–phonetic, with 心 identifying a state of feeling and 次 supplying the sound. Shirakawa interprets 次 through a person standing and lamenting; the related 姿 expresses that person's appearance.

He therefore proposes an earlier sense of lamenting freely according to one's feelings. The older dictionary gloss unrestrained records the more general stage: emotion or intention is allowed to follow its own course without restraint.

From this come acting at will, arbitrariness, indulgence, and leaving something to another's discretion. Shirakawa also compares 肆, another word for unrestrained behaviour, while giving that graph its own ritual history. The comparison concerns related sound and freedom from restraint; it does not turn the two characters into identical formations.
