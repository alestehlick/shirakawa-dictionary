遵
Uncategorized
Kun: shitagau, yoru  |  On: jun
to follow ・ to conform to ・ to rely on ・ to learn from

Explanation

遵 is semantic–phonetic, with 尊 supplying the sound in the relevant jun family. The movement element accompanies a word for following or going in accordance with something.

Shirakawa compares 循, whose formation he associates with military inspection using a shield. 遵, by contrast, is characteristically used for adherence to a rule or established standard, as in Mencius's language about following the laws of former kings. The two writings can nevertheless overlap in use.

He places 巡 and 徇 beside them as related vocabulary of following and inspection. The familiar sense obey or comply thus belongs to a wider group of words for guided movement, imitation, and adherence. It should not be derived merely from the independent modern meaning of 尊, respect.
