梗
Kun: yamanire, arai  |  On: kou
mountain elm ・ stiff or rough ・ to obstruct ・ outline or main points

Explanation

梗 is semantic–phonetic, with 木 specifying a tree and 更 supplying the sound kō. Shirakawa associates the phonetic family with obstruction or something that does not pass smoothly.

An older botanical gloss identifies a kind of mountain elm. He considers the tree's obstructive or thorny character relevant to its name, rather than interpreting the modern graph as a drawing of its branches. From this range come stiffness, roughness, and blocking.

The word-family includes the image of something caught in the throat and the related choking word 哽. These help explain the force of obstruction in later compounds. 梗概, an outline or account of the main points, belongs to the rough, preliminary side of the range: it presents the broad shape before fuller preparation or elaboration.
