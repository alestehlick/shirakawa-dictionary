𠮟
Kun: shikaru  |  On: shitsu
to scold ・ to rebuke ・ to shout a warning ・ a clicking sound of disapproval

Explanation

𠮟 is semantic–phonetic, with 口 specifying the voice and 七 supplying the sound shitsu. Shirakawa begins with the older definition of reprimanding or sharply reproaching someone.

He treats the words in 𠮟咤 as imitative of the force and tone of the utterance. The sound may stop another person's action, express a rebuke, or take the form of a disapproving click of the tongue.

The character can therefore name both the act of scolding and the sound produced in doing it. Its formation is organized around a vocal word and its phonetic; 七 is not seven people being addressed or seven repetitions of a warning. The Jōyō headword here preserves the distinct 𠮟 spelling.
