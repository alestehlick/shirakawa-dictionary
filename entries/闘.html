闘
Kun: tatakau  |  On: tou
to fight ・ battle ・ contest

Explanation

闘 abbreviates the fuller 鬭, combining 鬥 with 斲 as its sound element. 鬥 pictures two people grappling, their hands clutching at one another and their hair disordered. It is the older graph for fighting.

斲 brings an armed form of the same conflict: a notched shield held on one side and 斤, a battle-axe, on the other. The fuller character joins bodily struggle with the equipment of combat.

闘 therefore means to fight or contend, especially when opponents meet and engage directly. The related 戰 uses another shield, 單, with 戈, a weapon, to express armed fighting. Both preserve the practical means of conflict within the written form.
