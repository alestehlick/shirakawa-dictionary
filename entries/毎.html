毎
Kun: goto  |  On: mai
every ・ each

Explanation

毎 pictures a woman with her hair bound and adorned for ritual. The ancient forms emphasize the ornament above the head. Adding a hand produces 敏, concerned with preparing or attending to the adornment; adding threads gives 繁.

Bronze inscriptions use 毎 and 敏 for earnest application or diligence, especially in sacred duties. The familiar meanings ‘always,’ ‘each time,’ and ‘every’ are later sound borrowings. The woman’s ritual preparation explains the original form and its early activity, while the borrowed use accounts for the everyday word now written 毎.
