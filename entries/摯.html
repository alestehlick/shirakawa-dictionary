摯
Uncategorized
Kun: tsukamu, utsu  |  On: shi
to grasp firmly ・ to strike ・ earnest or thorough ・ ceremonial offering

Explanation

摯 is semantic–phonetic, using 執 as its phonetic and adding 手. Shirakawa interprets 執 as the application of handcuffs, giving the family an association with holding or binding firmly. He treats 摯 as a differentiated formation from 執 rather than accepting a purely compound-meaning analysis.

Its central action is a forceful grip; it can also mean striking or fierce treatment. Sound-based interchange with 贄 brings in a ceremonial gift, and related 質 concerns a firmly established pledge or agreement.

The earnest, thorough range familiar in 真摯 is also connected with 致 and 至, words for reaching or bringing something fully to its destination. Shirakawa thus distinguishes the gripping foundation from several related-word branches that contribute the character's later moral and ceremonial meanings.
