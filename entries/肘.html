肘
Kun: hiji, osaeru  |  On: chuu
elbow ・ arm joint ・ to press or restrain with the elbow ・ a measure of length

Explanation

肘 is semantic–phonetic, with 肉 indicating the body and 寸 supplying the chū sound. Shirakawa proposes that 寸 here abbreviates 丑, whose form concerns applying force with the fingertips and holding something tightly.

He rejects explaining the elbow through the wrist's pulse point, an older interpretation based on 寸: the wrist location does not account for the elbow. In his comparison, 丑 applies force with the fingers, whereas 肘 applies it at the arm's joint.

The word therefore names the elbow and can describe pressing, restraining, or stopping something with it. It also serves as a bodily measure of length. The proposed abbreviated phonetic and the anatomical classifier together organize his account of the joint and its force.
