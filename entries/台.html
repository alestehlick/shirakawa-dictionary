台
Kun: utena, dai  |  On: dai, tai
elevated sacred building ・ platform ・ support or stand ・ court or official establishment

Explanation

For a platform or elevated building, 台 replaces the older 臺. 臺 combines an abbreviated 高, a tall gate-like structure, with 至, an arrow reaching its destination. A sacred arrow was shot to select the site of an important building; 至 marks the chosen place.

臺 names the high structure erected on that consecrated site. A grain-shaped ornament on the roof further marked its sacred character. Such towers were places where a divine presence could gather. From the elevated building come a platform, a stand, or a high, level area of ground. The court and its offices also acquired titles drawn from this monumental setting.

The simpler 台 originally belonged to separate words and rites. Its modern substitution for 臺 preserves the word’s use while concealing the tall building and the arrow-selected site in the older graph.
