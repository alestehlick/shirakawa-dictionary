台
Uncategorized
Kun: utena, dai  |  On: dai, tai
elevated sacred building ・ platform ・ support or stand ・ court or official establishment

Explanation

For the platform meaning, modern 台 replaces 臺. Shirakawa reads 臺 through a tall gate-like building and 至, which he interprets as an arrow used to select and claim a site. The rooftop bears a grain-shaped ritual ornament.

The building is a sacred elevated place where divine presence can gather. Monumental towers in historical accounts preserve this ceremonial setting. From the elevated structure come a platform, a stand, and a high, level piece of ground; court and official titles extend its institutional range.

Shirakawa stresses that the simpler graph 台 originally had separate histories. One concerned an invocation for fertility, while an i-reading belonged to the 怡 family. Its present use for 臺 is a later written substitution, so the five-stroke form alone cannot explain the tower's origin.
