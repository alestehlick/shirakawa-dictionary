神
Kun: kami, kan, ko  |  On: shin, jin
god ・ deity ・ spirit

Explanation

神 joins 示, the altar or offering table, with 申 as its phonetic. 申 pictures lightning, a sudden flash understood as the manifestation of a heavenly deity's power. It was the earlier writing for that divine presence.

As 申 acquired other uses, including saying or reporting, 示 was added to distinguish the sacred word. The lightning shape and altar together make the deity explicit: a power encountered in awe and approached through worship.

神 names deities and spiritual presences in nature and the ancestral world. Their power can inspire fear or anger as well as bestow blessing. The word also extends to inward spirit and extraordinary mental efficacy. Across these uses, the visible sign of lightning evokes an otherwise unseen force.
