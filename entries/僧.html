僧
Kun:  |  On: sou
buddhist monk ・ priest

Explanation

僧 combines 人, a person, with the sound element 曾. 曾 pictures a grain steamer, the implement later written 甑 and called koshiki in Japanese. In 僧, its role is to supply the sound.

The character belongs to the transcription 僧伽, representing Sanskrit saṅgha, the Buddhist community. 僧 is the shortened form of that transcription. It came to name a person practising the Buddhist teaching, especially a member of the monastic community—a monk or priest. The meaning therefore comes from the Buddhist word being transcribed, while 人 identifies the human referent and 曾 supplies the required sound.
