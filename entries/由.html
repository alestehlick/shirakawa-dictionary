由
Kun: yoshi, yoru  |  On: yu, yuu, yui
reason ・ cause ・ origin ・ to be due to

Explanation

由 probably goes back to a hollow gourd. Its ripe interior has dissolved, leaving the shell empty. The shape resembles 卣, the bronze wine vessel, which may preserve the older writing.

This hollow interior accompanies several related formations. 油 concerns the oily condition of the ripened contents, while words for a tube or an axis develop other features of the form. The family gathers the vessel, its contents, and the space within it.

The familiar uses of 由 are sound loans: following a course, the place from which something proceeds, using something, and a reason or cause. From the course followed come origin and circumstances, as in 由来 and 由縁. These linguistic uses continue alongside the older material image.
