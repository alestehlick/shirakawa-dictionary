苛
Kun: karai, kibishii  |  On: ka
harsh ・ severe ・ exacting and troublesome ・ to reproach

Explanation

苛 is semantic–phonetic, with 艸 and the phonetic 可, ka. Shirakawa notes a disagreement in the lexical tradition: one version defines a small plant, while a quotation of the same dictionary gives exceptional severity.

He relates 可 here to reproaching or scolding, as in 呵 and 訶. The word can therefore concern severe treatment, fault-finding, and demands made in troublesome detail.

Shirakawa also compares 苦 and related vocabulary, suggesting a movement from the sharp or unpleasant taste of a plant to the state of a person's mind or conduct. This is a proposed sensory-to-human development. It allows the plant classifier and the harshness meanings to be considered together without pretending that the early lexical evidence is entirely uniform.
