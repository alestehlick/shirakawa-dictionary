録
Kun:  |  On: roku
record ・ to record ・ to write down ・ to engrave

Explanation

録, formerly 錄, combines 金, metal, with 彔 as its sound element. 彔 pictures an awl-like tool boring into wood, with chips scattering from the hole. It brings an association of cutting or incising a surface.

With 金, the work is done in metal, especially bronze. 録 means engraving or inscribing something so that it remains recorded on the object.

The lasting inscription gives the general sense of making a record. Writing something in a document, copying it, or entering it in an account continues the act of preserving information through marks. The medium broadens, while the movement from incision to an enduring record remains clear.
