報
Kun: mukuiru, shiraseru  |  On: hou
to inform ・ to report ・ to recompense ・ to retaliate

Explanation

報 combines 幸 with 𠬝. 幸 pictures a restraint fastened around the hands. In 𠬝, a hand presses down a kneeling person, the posture of enforced submission also found in 服. Together they show an offender held under restraint as punishment for a wrong.

The punishment returns an act upon the person responsible, giving repayment or retribution. The same relation works for a benefit: kindness can be answered with gratitude, service, or a reward. Bronze inscriptions already use 報 in this wider sphere of making a return, including repayment to the ancestors for their virtue.

An answer can also take the form of words. From responding to what one has received, 報 extends to reporting, announcing, and conveying news.
