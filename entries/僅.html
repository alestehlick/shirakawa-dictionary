僅
Kun: wazuka, wazukani  |  On: kin
scant ・ only a little ・ barely ・ just managed

Explanation

僅 is semantic–phonetic, with the kin phonetic also found in 饉 and 勤. Shirakawa traces that element through an older formation depicting the burning of a ritual medium during famine, placing the family in the context of hunger and emergency rites.

Although the old dictionary gloss concerns barely managing to do something, he considers a scant grain harvest the more concrete starting point. A classical commentary describes a year in which there was only just a crop.

From the small yield, 僅 extends to limited quantity, low degree, and a condition achieved with difficulty. Hence the familiar barely or only just. Shirakawa's unfolding keeps the famine-related background before the general adverb, rather than reading scarcity as a picture of an isolated person.
