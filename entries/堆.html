堆
Kun: oka, uzutakai  |  On: tai, tsui
heap ・ mound ・ to pile up

Explanation

堆 is semantic–phonetic, with 土 indicating earth and 隹 providing the sound tai. Its central meanings concern heaping earth and the small mound produced by that action.

Shirakawa disputes identifying an older graph resembling 阜 as the original picture of this mound. He interprets that older graph as an early form of 師, showing a large folded piece of meat, while he explains 阜 itself as a ladder used in divine ascent and descent. Neither should therefore be substituted casually for the formation of 堆.

He places 堆 beside related words for accumulated or raised ground, including 敦. Where a sound element has no separate, identifiable semantic contribution, he also allows the possibility of an imitative sound behind the word. The secure organizing sense here remains earth gathered into a heap.
