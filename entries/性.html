性
Kun: saga  |  On: sei, sho
nature ・ character ・ sex

Explanation

性 combines 忄, the heart, with 生 as its phonetic. 生 concerns being born or coming into life. Together the word names what a being has by its birth: its inherent nature, disposition, or constitution.

The same sound relationship appears in 姓, a person's lineage or origin. 性 concerns the qualities that belong to something, while 姓 concerns where a person comes from.

性 is not restricted to human feelings or even to living things. Classical texts speak of the 性 of land and of water, meaning their characteristic properties. Human temperament is one application of this wider idea of a nature belonging to its bearer.
