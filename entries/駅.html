駅
Kun: umaya, ekisha  |  On: eki
relay station ・ relay horse or carriage ・ station

Explanation

駅 is the modern form of 驛, combining 馬, horse, with 睪 as its sound element. 睪 pictures an animal carcass and carries associations of loosening, unfolding, and continuing at length. In 驛, that continuation becomes travel carried onward through a succession of mounts or vehicles.

Horses were kept ready for a relay service. At each post, the traveller or message could be transferred to a fresh horse and continue along the route. 驛 names this relay transport and its stations.

The familiar railway station continues the place where a journey is sustained and passed onward. The modern 尺 is an abbreviation of the older right side; the transport meaning is clearest in the fuller 馬 and 睪 form.
