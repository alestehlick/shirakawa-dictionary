駅
Uncategorized
Kun: umaya, ekisha  |  On: eki
relay station ・ relay horse or carriage ・ station

Explanation

駅 is the modern form of 驛. Shirakawa analyzes the fuller graph as semantic–phonetic, with 馬 identifying the horse and 睪 supplying the sound eki. The modern 尺 shape is a simplification and should not be assigned an independent measuring meaning here.

He interprets 睪 through an animal carcass and the ideas of loosening, unfolding, and continuing at length. In 驛 that continuing sequence is the succession of horses or carriages used in a relay. The older definition concerns horses kept ready for that service.

A relay station is consequently part of a route along which travel and messages are carried onward by changes of mount. Shirakawa compares 繹 and 訳 within the same phonetic family, emphasizing continuation. The familiar station word preserves this transport history even when horses are no longer involved.
