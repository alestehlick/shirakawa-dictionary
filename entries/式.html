式
Kun:  |  On: shiki
rite ・ style ・ standard

Explanation

式 combines 工, a sacred implement held in the ritual specialist's left hand, with 弋, a corded arrow used for catching birds. The arrow was probably also employed as a ritual implement.

Together the objects protect what is sacred and drive away harmful influences, restoring a proper condition. The action follows a prescribed procedure, giving the connection with a form or standard to be observed.

From performing that procedure come using, following a model, and making something a rule for conduct. Related 拭 concerns wiping or cleansing; 試 and 弑 preserve other actions carried out through ritual means. The familiar pattern or ceremony retains the idea that the act is effective when performed in its proper form.
