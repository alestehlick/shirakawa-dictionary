諧
Uncategorized
Kun: au, totonou, yawaragu  |  On: kai
to accord ・ to harmonize ・ to become gentle ・ jest or pleasantry

Explanation

諧 combines 言 with 皆 as the phonetic kai. Shirakawa interprets 皆 through invocations welcoming deities who descend together; the related 偕 retains the idea of accompanying one another.

諧 expresses agreement with divine intention and the harmonious condition that results. The older definition of fitting or meeting accords with this ritual starting point in his account. From such accord come adjustment, gentleness, and harmonious relations.

Shirakawa compares other harmony words, including 和, while explicitly distinguishing their graphic origins. Similar sound and meaning do not prove identical pictures or rites. The playful or jesting range is also recorded, but his principal unfolding follows an invocation that accords with the gods into the broader sense of things fitting together peacefully.
