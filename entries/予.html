予
Kun: arakajime  |  On: yo
in advance ・ beforehand ・ i

Explanation

In its use for ‘beforehand,’ 予 is the modern form of 豫. 豫 combines 象, elephant, with 予 as its sound element. It may preserve a practice of using elephants to divine future events, although that practice cannot now be verified. Seeking knowledge of what is to come would give the sense of acting in advance.

豫 also has old uses for ease, enjoyment, or a relaxed condition. In 猶予, that unhurried state becomes hesitation or delay in reaching a decision. The senses gathered under the modern 予 therefore include both advance preparation and lingering before an action.

The simpler 予 has a separate origin as a shuttle-shaped graph and can also write the first-person pronoun. Its use for 豫 is the modern simplification.
