役
Kun:  |  On: yaku, eki
service ・ duty ・ role ・ military service ・ corvee labor

Explanation

役 combines 彳, movement along a road, with 殳, a hand-held staff or weapon. The implement could bear feathers and serve as a ritual staff carried on a circuit or patrol.

The same equipment was probably used when going on campaign or undertaking frontier guard duty. An early definition gives guarding the border, placing the character within the obligations of military service. Movement with the staff joins ritual patrol to practical watchkeeping.

From this service come work, a duty, an assigned task, and the person performing it. The word can also mean employing someone or serving another. Its general occupational range develops from a particular kind of public obligation.
