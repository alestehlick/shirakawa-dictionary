階
Kun: kizahashi  |  On: kai
stairs ・ steps ・ floor ・ rank

Explanation

階 combines 阜, the divine ladder, with 皆 as its sound element. 皆 shows divine presences descending side by side in answer to prayer. 阜 supplies the structure by which they move between heaven and the place of worship.

階 probably first names the steps prepared for that descent, or the stairway of an altar. Their ordered sequence allows movement from one level to another.

From sacred steps, the word broadens to stairs and ladders generally, then to stages, grades, and ranks. A position in a hierarchy is one level in such a sequence, and advancement is movement upward through it. The ordered descent of the original rite becomes the familiar arrangement of successive levels.
