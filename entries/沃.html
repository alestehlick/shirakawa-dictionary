沃
Uncategorized
Kun: sosogu, utsukushii  |  On: yoku
to pour water ・ to wash ・ to irrigate ・ fertile or luxuriant

Explanation

沃 is semantic–phonetic, with 夭 as the phonetic in the simpler form. Shirakawa also discusses a fuller writing using a related sound element.

Although the traditional definition concerns irrigation, he considers ritual handwashing the earlier setting. A passage in the Rites of Zhou describes an attendant pouring water for the king to wash during a major ceremony. Pouring, washing, and moistening are therefore closely connected.

Clean, well-moistened things can appear glossy, fresh, and abundant. Shirakawa follows that quality through a poetic description of flourishing leaves and then to the fertile land expressed in 沃土 and 沃野. His sequence runs from pouring and purification to lustre and richness, before the familiar agricultural sense becomes central.
