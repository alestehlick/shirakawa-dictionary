訃
Kun: shirase  |  On: fu
to announce a death ・ death notice ・ to go and report

Explanation

訃 is semantic–phonetic, using 卜 for the sound fu, also found in 赴 and 仆. 言 identifies the act of reporting. The character should not be explained solely through the independent meaning divination of 卜.

Shirakawa cites a funeral-ritual instruction in which a messenger goes to announce that a named retainer has died. Different textual versions write either 赴 or 訃 for the reporting action.

The death notice is thus connected with going to deliver news, and the speech classifier makes that communicative function explicit. Shirakawa treats the relation with 赴 as sound-based borrowing and conventional differentiation. The familiar 訃報 retains the specific report of death rather than the wider movement meaning of its related writing.
