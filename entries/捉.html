捉
Uncategorized
Kun: toraeru, toru  |  On: soku, saku
to catch ・ to seize ・ to grasp ・ to hold

Explanation

捉 is semantic–phonetic, with 手 identifying manual action and 足 supplying the sound soku. Shirakawa compares 促, pursuing or pressing from behind: 捉 is reaching the object of pursuit and taking hold of it.

The older definitions use words for gripping and holding. He notes their sound relationships as well as their similar actions, distinguishing pursuit followed by seizure from squeezing tightly or folding something into the hand.

The central sense is therefore active capture and grasp. The foot component chiefly participates in the phonetic construction and the associated pursuing vocabulary; it is not necessary to imagine literally grabbing a person's foot in every use. The later general meaning of apprehending something continues that movement toward a successful hold.
