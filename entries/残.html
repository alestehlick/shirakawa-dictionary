残
Kun: nokoru, nokosu, sokonau  |  On: zan
to remain ・ to leave behind ・ to spoil ・ cruel

Explanation

残, formerly 殘, combines 歹 with the sound element 戔. 歹 represents the bones left from the upper part of a body. 戔 consists of two 戈 and carries the sense of small or thin things lying one over another. Together they suggest scattered, scant remains of the body.

What survives after destruction gives 残 its central sense: a remainder, something left behind, or something that has not yet been used up. The same image also looks back toward the injury that produced the remains. It can therefore express harming or destroying, and the cruelty of such treatment. Ordinary ‘remaining’ and the harshness of 残酷 or 残虐 belong to these two aspects of what is left after damage.
