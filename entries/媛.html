媛
Kun: hime  |  On: en
beautiful woman ・ graceful woman ・ hime

Explanation

媛 is semantic–phonetic, with 女 identifying a woman and 爰 supplying the sound en. The older definition calls her beautiful and offers an explanation involving someone drawn toward her.

Shirakawa instead suggests that the word began as a description of appearance or bearing, comparable to its use in a descriptive compound. He relates it to 宛, 婉, 円, and other words conveying roundedness, fullness, and gentle grace.

The resulting range includes a beautiful or graceful woman and the Japanese hime. His brief entry does not reconstruct a separate royal ceremony or claim that the graph originally designated a princess's rank. It unfolds the established female designation through the phonetic family and its qualities of soft, rounded beauty.
