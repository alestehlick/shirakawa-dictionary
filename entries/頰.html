頰
Kun: hoo  |  On: kyou
cheek ・ side of the face ・ pleasant by related usage

Explanation

頰 is semantic–phonetic, with 頁 identifying the head or face and 夾 supplying the sound kyō. Shirakawa interprets 夾 through things placed on both sides of a central object.

The cheeks are correspondingly the two sides of the face. The older dictionary's definition face-side gives the anatomical meaning directly, and related words such as 挟 and 脅 preserve the bilateral association in the sound-family.

Shirakawa discusses an earlier scholar's grouping of several such phonetics but cautions that some similar bilateral words belong to a different family. The cheek explanation is therefore specific, not a universal rule for every matching sound. A pleasant or comfortable use is also recorded through interchange with a related writing.
